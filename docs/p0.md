# P0 partially observed CartPole protocol

The task uses Gymnax CartPole-v1 dynamics, reward and 500-step horizon. The
policy sees cart position and pole angle; cart and angular velocities are
zeroed in its four-value observation. The evaluator, candidate selection,
state carry and train/validation/held-out RNG namespaces remain shared with
the existing control task. This makes temporal inference useful without a
new simulator dependency.

The [frozen adaptive manifest](../configs/p0-partial-cartpole-acceptance-v1.json)
specifies five evolution seeds, 100 generations, eight training episodes per
generation, 32 validation episodes, 100 paired held-out episodes per final
policy, eight finalists, a 7,200-second per-seed budget and a 64 MiB rollout
estimate limit. The direct, fixed-substrate and weight-only manifests use the
same task, seed, episode and held-out protocol, while retaining their declared
evolution operators and substrate settings. Each requires its own matching
G9 pilot.

The random-action baseline uses the exact held-out reset keys and a separate
random action stream. Its frozen 100-episode means for seeds 0–4 are 22.64,
23.67, 21.14, 21.44 and 19.61 steps. The positive normalization scale is
500 steps. `scripts/p0_report.py` computes each seed's mean paired policy
improvement divided by 500, then bootstraps the median using evolution seeds
as the independent units. The research target is median improvement >=0.20
and a positive lower endpoint of the 95% bootstrap interval. The per-seed
raw target of 120 steps and required-seed count are reported alongside it as
diagnostics; they do not add a third requirement to the roadmap's P0 quality
gate.
The frozen acceptance run is measured even if it misses this target.

The [completed comparison](../benchmarks/P0-COMPARISON-REPORT.md) found the
weight-only method passes the harder-task quality target in five seeds.
Adaptive EMR, direct genomes and fixed CPPN did not reach the 20% median
gain under their frozen configurations. This is a system comparison across
each method's declared architecture, not an encoding-isolating ablation.
