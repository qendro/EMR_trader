# S0 adaptive packing and routing

The public EMR substrate still supplies compact per-genome phenotypes with
full-grid indices. `index_map` makes the compact-to-grid and grid-to-compact
mapping explicit and rejects duplicate, unordered or out-of-range positions.
`BucketFamily` declares a fixed, finite hidden-width family from the task's
node capacity. A phenotype is padded only to its own bucket. Padded hidden
slots have zero weights, zero bias and a false node mask, so they do not alter
carried hidden state. The compact phenotype remains the archived/exported
artifact.

`route` groups execution plans by `(inputs, hidden width, outputs)` and keeps
the original candidate indices. The runner evaluates each group through its
existing task evaluator, then scatters episode scores and validity back to
those indices before validation or native tell. Candidate IDs, scenario RNG
keys, fitness order and final selection are unchanged. The bucket family is
chosen once per adapter and has a bounded signature count; promotion requires
an explicit larger node capacity and preserves all earlier width routes.
No silent capacity growth or edge truncation is performed.

The [S0 tests](../tests/test_packing.py) cover reversible maps, ordered
compaction, padding across two carried steps, permutation-safe routing,
capacity refusal and stable routes after promotion. The A2 evolution test
executes a real decoded population in its routed buckets and tells the native
evolver. The development memory and CartPole smokes produced the same
three-generation quality histories as their pre-S0 versions. A full
cross-backend and longer compilation audit remains part of M4 validation.
